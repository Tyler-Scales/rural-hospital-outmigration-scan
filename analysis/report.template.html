What it's worth under CAH payment rules

Change in annual operating margin, low to high volume step

Bars run from the low to the high volume step, and the tick marks the middle step. The open circle shows the middle step for swing beds if MA pays $487 a day.

Traditional Medicare pays a CAH 101% of cost, so added Medicare volume pays its share of the fixed costs the hospital carries anyway. One more Traditional Medicare swing day adds {{tm_day}} to margin, and one more acute day adds {{acute_tm_day}}.

Medicare Advantage pays its contract rate. An MA swing day adds {{ma_day_interim}} at $3,180 a day (about 95% of the interim rate), and {{ma_day_placeholder_phrase}} at $487. MA acute days use a $3,500 placeholder and add {{acute_ma_day}}.

Outpatient volume lowers the cost-to-charge ratio. That trims what Medicare pays for inpatient ancillaries, and the engine counts it. At the $487 MA rate, the model would need non-Medicare outpatient to pay {{op_yield_placeholder}} of charges to match the hospital's books; at $3,180 it needs {{op_yield_interim}}. These figures use $3,180.

Assumptions: swing stays last {{swing_los}} days (the county's Medicare skilled-nursing average), and {{swing_tm_share}} of them are Traditional Medicare, matching the hospital's {{fy}} mix. Acute stays last {{acute_los}} days, with {{acute_tm_share}} Traditional Medicare. 60% of ancillary cost and 75% of nursing-unit cost are treated as fixed. Lever costs (software, staff time) are not netted out; the workbook has a column for them.

Data that would sharpen this

Each of these is a standard report export. Counts by month, service and destination are enough; no patient names are needed.

  • Outgoing referrals and orders by type and destination: sizes move 2.
  • Swing-bed referrals received, accepted and declined, by referring hospital and payer: sizes move 1.
  • ED visits by acuity and hour, and transfers out by reason: sizes moves 3 and 5.
  • Call volume, abandoned calls and third-next-available appointment for the clinic: sizes move 3.
  • MA contract rates for swing and acute care: decides whether move 1 should include MA patients.
